Not sure? Look up your address once on the council's bin collection calendar, then tap the matching dates here. This page works out every week after that.

Worked out from Merri-bek's four-bin cycle: food and garden every week, recycling every second week, glass every four weeks, and rubbish every week (every second week in trial streets).

Bins are collected on public holidays except Christmas Day, New Year's Day, Good Friday and Anzac Day. In those weeks this page moves collections from the holiday onwards a day later, as the council did over Christmas 2024.

Rubbish moves to every second week across Merri-bek from mid-2027, so this page will need an update then.
